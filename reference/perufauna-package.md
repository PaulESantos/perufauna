# perufauna: Easily Load and Harmonize Peruvian Fauna and Biodiversity Packages

The `perufauna` metapackage streamlines the installation, loading, and
integration of specialized R packages focused on Peruvian fauna and
biodiversity:

- `avesperu`: Taxonomic backbone and search tools for the birds of Peru
  (UNOP checklist).

- `perumammals`: Taxonomic backbone and validation for mammals of Peru
  (Pacheco et al. 2021).

- `citesperu`: Status and verification of species in CITES Peru
  appendices (MINAM).

- `perufaunads004`: Threatened fauna categorization according to D.S. N°
  004-2014-MINAGRI and SERFOR Red Book.

In addition to orchestrated attachment, `perufauna` provides integrated
cross-referencing functions such as
[`pf_match`](https://paulesantos.github.io/perufauna/reference/pf_match.md)
and
[`pf_status`](https://paulesantos.github.io/perufauna/reference/pf_status.md)
to analyze species across all taxonomic and regulatory sources in a
single call.
